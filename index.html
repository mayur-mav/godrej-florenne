— THE PROJECT —

Godrej Florenne Whitefield,
Project Overview

An exclusive enclave of 242 French-inspired private residences set across 20 lush acres with private gardens, terraces, and dedicated in-home elevators. Conceived by master architect Hafeez Contractor with classic European design, a 22,000 sq ft central clubhouse, and an address on the high-growth Soukya Road corridor, minutes from the Satellite Town Ring Road.

A French-inspired garden sanctuary: 62% of the 20-acre campus is dedicated open landscape, planned as biophilic private gardens, internal courtyards, and tree-lined avenues rather than leftover setbacks.
G+3 four-level structure with private elevator: Ground-floor car porch and living, upper private bedroom suites, private in-home elevator serving all floors, and an expansive rooftop entertainment terrace with pergola.
Four ways to live here: from a 3,725 sq ft 4 BHK Optima to an expansive 5,524 sq ft 5 BHK Luxe, each four-level residence featuring private open-to-sky courtyards and dedicated family lounges.
A central clubhouse of about 22,000 sq ft: indoor sports, fitness studio, multi-cuisine dining, spa, and squash court alongside a resort-style infinity swimming pool, tennis court, and celebration lawns.
The high-growth corridor of Whitefield: located off Soukya Road, just 1 km to NH 648 (STRR), 7.4 km to Whitefield Railway Station, 9 km to Kadugodi Tree Park Metro, and 15 mins to ITPL and EPIP tech corridor.
